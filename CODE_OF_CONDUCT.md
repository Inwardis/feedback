# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Inwardis community to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other members.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at
**conduct@inwardis.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for
behaviors they deem inappropriate.
